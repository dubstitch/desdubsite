03 / How I think

Structure is a love language.
It makes room for magic.

Structure is how I show care for the people using an experience and for the people who maintain it. But structure alone isn’t the whole picture. I bring development, content, design and everything I’ve learned together, so the pieces work beautifully as one. The system should be thoughtful enough to support the human experience, not get in its way.

UnderstandFind the user problem and the business need.
OrganizeGive complexity a usable structure.
ImplementMake the experience real within the platform.
RefineTest, measure and improve what matters.
